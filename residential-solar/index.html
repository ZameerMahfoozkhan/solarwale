Residential Rooftop Solar for Homes in Uttar Pradesh

Clean, reliable solar power for independent houses and duplexes. Generate your own electricity, protect against rising tariffs, and benefit from government rooftop subsidies.

Home Solutions

Slash Your Domestic Power Bills by up to 90%

Rising electricity tariffs in Uttar Pradesh make domestic air conditioning and appliance usage increasingly expensive. With a residential on-grid solar system, your rooftop becomes a miniature power station that covers your daytime power demand and exports surplus electricity to the grid.

Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential property owners in Uttar Pradesh can receive up to ₹78,000 in Central Government subsidy plus additional UP State assistance, dramatically lowering your initial investment.

Frequently Asked Questions

For a 3 BHK home with 1-2 air conditioners and a monthly electricity bill between ₹3,000 and ₹4,500, a 3 kW or 4 kW on-grid solar system is typically ideal.
A standard on-grid system automatically disconnects during grid outages for utility lineman safety (anti-islanding). If backup during power cuts is needed, we recommend an intelligent Hybrid Solar System with lithium or tubular battery storage.
