Frequently Asked Questions in Prayagraj

Common questions from property owners in Prayagraj (Allahabad) regarding solar installation and net metering.

The cost of rooftop solar in Prayagraj depends on system capacity (typically 3 kW to 10 kW for homes) and structure type. A standard 3 kW residential on-grid system generally ranges between ₹1,80,000 to ₹2,20,000 before government assistance. With the PM Surya Ghar central subsidy of ₹78,000 plus UP state assistance, the effective homeowner expense is substantially lower.
In Prayagraj, net metering is facilitated through Purvanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd (PVVNL). Once Solar Wallah installs the rooftop system, a joint inspection is performed with electricity board engineers and a bidirectional meter is connected. Solar power is consumed first by your property, and surplus units flow to the grid, offsetting your electricity bills.

Site surveys in Prayagraj are scheduled within 24 to 48 hours. Physical mounting on your roof takes 2 to 3 days, followed by DISCOM net meter synchronization.
